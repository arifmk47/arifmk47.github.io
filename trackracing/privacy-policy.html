TrackRacingGame: Privacy policy

Last updated: 25 November 2026

This policy explains what information the TrackRacingGame app for Android ("the app") collects and how it is used. The app is made by an independent developer (GitHub: arifmk47), referred to here as "we".

Information stored on your device

The app keeps your game progress and settings (for example levels completed, best scores, coins, sound and vibration settings) on your device only. We do not run servers, we do not have user accounts, and this data is never sent to us. Uninstalling the app or clearing its data deletes it.

Advertising

The app shows ads through Google AdMob, a service of Google LLC. To serve and measure ads, and to prevent fraud, the Google Mobile Ads SDK may collect and process:

  • your device's advertising ID (Android advertising ID) and app set ID,
  • device and app information (such as device model, operating system version, app version, language),
  • your IP address and approximate location derived from it,
  • information about your interactions with ads (such as views and taps),
  • diagnostic and performance information about the ads.

This data is collected and processed by Google, not by us. Ads may be personalised based on this information where the law allows and, where required, only with your consent. You can learn more in How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and the Google Privacy Policy.

You can reset your advertising ID or opt out of ads personalisation in your device's settings (Settings > Google > Ads, or Settings > Privacy > Ads, depending on your device).

Consent (EEA, UK and Switzerland, and other regions where required)

Where required by law, the app asks for your consent before any personalised ads are shown, using Google's User Messaging Platform. You can change your choice at any time with the Privacy options button on the level select screen.

Rewarded ads

Some ads are optional: you can choose to watch one in return for an in-game reward (for example double coins). Watching them is never required to play.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

Data sharing and selling

We do not sell your personal information and we do not receive it. The only third party involved is Google, as described above.

Security and retention

Data kept on your device stays there until you delete it. Data collected by Google is retained according to Google's own policies.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct or delete your personal data, or to object to its processing. Because we hold no personal data, requests about advertising data are best made to Google through its privacy tools; you can also withdraw ad consent at any time with Privacy options in the app.

Changes

We may update this policy. The date at the top shows the latest version.

Contact

For questions about this policy, contact the developer at the email address listed on the app's Google Play page.
